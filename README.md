# Taiwan Tourism Analytics for Hotel Investment Decision

## Project Background

This project simulates a real-world business scenario where the CEO of a hotel group is considering investing in the Taiwanese market.

The objective is to analyze inbound tourism data to support strategic decision-making regarding hotel expansion.

---

## Business Objective

To provide data-driven insights on:

* Who visits Taiwan (visitor composition)
* Why they visit (travel purpose)
* When they visit (seasonality)
* Where they come from (regional distribution)

These insights help evaluate market demand and identify potential opportunities for hotel investment.

---

## Key Insights

* **Asia dominates the market**, contributing approximately 88% of total visitors.
* **Leisure travel is the primary purpose**, indicating strong demand for tourism-related accommodation.
* **Clear seasonal patterns** exist, with peak demand during specific months.
* Emerging markets such as **Oceania show higher growth rates**, suggesting future expansion opportunities.

---

## Business Implications

* Hotels should focus on **Asian tourist segments** for initial market entry.
* Investment strategies should consider **seasonal pricing and capacity planning**.
* Opportunities exist to target **high-growth regions** for long-term expansion.
* Product offerings can be tailored to **leisure travelers**, such as vacation packages.
